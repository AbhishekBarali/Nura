# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Physicians during patient visits. Using Nura in real-time during consultations to eliminate manual documentation. Context: time-pressured clinical environment where every second with the patient matters. Built for a hackathon demonstrating autonomous clinical AI.

## Product Purpose

Nura is an autonomous clinical voice agent that listens to doctor-patient conversations and independently detects drug interactions, flags allergy conflicts, routes referrals, and generates structured SOAP notes — in real-time. It exists to eliminate the 49% of physician workday spent on documentation and reduce burnout-driven turnover.

Success looks like: a physician presses one button, conducts a normal patient visit, and walks away with a complete chart update, referrals sent, and appointments booked — without touching a keyboard.

## Brand Personality

**Confident, Clinical, Minimal.**

- Voice: Direct and authoritative, like a senior colleague who handles things without being asked.
- Tone: Calm certainty. Never anxious, never over-explaining. The agent acts, then reports.
- Emotional goal: The physician should feel relief and trust — "it just handled that."

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS dashboards** (Salesforce-style) — bloated, cluttered, overwhelming with options
- **Consumer health/wellness apps** (MyFitnessPal, Calm) — playful colors, gamification, casual tone inappropriate for clinical context
- **Old-school EHR chrome** — no gray toolbars, no nested tabs, no 2005-era form density

## Design Principles

1. **Autonomy over interaction** — Nura acts without asking. The UI shows results, not options.
2. **Confidence through restraint** — Fewer elements, more trust. If it's on screen, it matters.
3. **Time is clinical** — Every UI decision respects that the user is with a patient. Zero cognitive overhead.
4. **Show the work, not the effort** — Display agent actions as accomplished facts, not loading states.
5. **Clinical precision** — Data is king. Labels are exact, alerts are unambiguous, nothing is decorative without purpose.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA compliance (contrast, focus management, screen reader support)
- Sufficient color contrast for clinical environments with variable lighting
- No information conveyed by color alone (icons + labels for all alert types)
